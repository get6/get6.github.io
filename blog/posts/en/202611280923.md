As a kid, you said whatever came to mind. As an adult, you check **what others think** first. I thought it was just me, but it seems most adults live this way. This post is encouragement for anyone who holds themselves back over what others think, and for me too.

## As adults, we start with what others think

Lately I've been disappointed in myself. I wasn't like this as a kid, but now I feel **listless** and I don't have much to laugh about.

I used to say whatever came to mind. Now the thing I care about most is "**What will other people think of me?**" I check whether I look strange, and I worry that I might be standing out.

But I don't think it's just me. It feels like most adults carry a similar worry around. If you're disappointed in who you are right now, the first thing I want to tell you is that **you're not the only one**.

## If you hold yourself back because you might look strange

Caring about what others think isn't the saddest part. What comes next is. Because we might look strange, **we stop ourselves from acting**.

Nobody told us not to. We stop ourselves first. So maybe you laugh less not because you became a boring person. Maybe you've just been holding back the fun version of you.

## Being creative means not caring about certain things

To be **creative**, there are things you have to stop caring about. The worry about looking strange or standing out is exactly that.

Back when we were kids saying whatever came to mind, we didn't weigh any of it. And if you weren't like this as a kid, it also means you weren't always this way.

## Let's be more creative and more fun

I want my life from here on to be **a lot more fun**. I hope you want that for yours too.

Being disappointed in who you are now means you still want a more fun life. I wrote about not putting a period on that disappointment in [How to Deal with Failure, Use a Comma Instead of a Period](/posts/202611242359).

It's okay to look strange. Let's be more creative and more fun.
